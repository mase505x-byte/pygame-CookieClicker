# Cookie Clicker

Cookie Clicker is a simple incremental game where the player clicks a cookie to earn points and then uses those points to buy upgrades and buildings that automatically generate more cookies.

## How to Play

- Click the cookie to increase your total cookie count.
- Spend cookies on upgrades and buildings to increase your production.
- Keep growing your cookie empire as fast as possible.
- Aim for higher totals and unlock stronger automation.

## Goal

The objective is to produce as many cookies as possible by combining manual clicking with strategic upgrades and purchases.
